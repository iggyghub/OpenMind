# Short basket tuning: pre-registered plan (written 2026-09-29, BEFORE any results)

Goal (future use, can't short yet): the numbers that best fit shorting when the trend is down,
chosen WITHOUT fooling ourselves.

Grid (24 combinations): switch-to-short level 40% or 45% (refill while below 50%),
stop 8/12/16/20% (rise above the lowest close), max hold 5/10/20 days. Everything else as in
SHORT-BASKET-PLAN.md.

Method: pick ONE combination using 2005-2015 only, by the short account's growth per year. Then run
that one combination once on 2016-2026, which the choice never saw.
Pass: in 2016-2026 the short account grows > 0%/yr (beats sitting in cash during down-trends) AND
the average bet is > 0. Also reported: where the 2005-2015 winner ranks in 2016-2026 (if the ranking
reshuffles, the tuning is noise). Any later variant is labelled post-hoc.
